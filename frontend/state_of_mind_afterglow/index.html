ABOUT US

"A brain shall never be restricted to specific data."

State of Mind was created to solve a quiet fatigue: scrolling endlessly through algorithmic feeds designed to maximize screen time rather than honor how you actually feel. We replace opaque black-box recommendations with a transparent, weighted synaptic brain that maps human emotional headspaces directly to artistic genres.

No sign-up, no algorithmic bubble, and zero third-party API dependencies at query time. Everything runs off a cloud-cached MySQL brain, refreshed periodically by autonomous harvesters, so recommendations are instant, resilient, and deeply resonant.
